## Unverified

1. **Exact motor, coupling and thrust-bearing fit.** The installed STEP geometry matches its local records, but those records do not identify verified dimensions for the owned 42SHDC3026-15B. The cited motor page explicitly describes the sibling 42SHDC3025-24B. Motor shaft length 24 mm and usable M3 depth 4.5 mm remain estimates. The new screws consume the full modeled 4.5 mm tapped depth, leaving no nominal depth margin. The [VXB coupling listing](https://vxb.com/products/4-pack-5mm-rigid-flange-shaft-couplings-with-8-scr), reopened today, supports 5 mm bore, 10 mm hub diameter/height, 22 mm flange and M3 holes; it does not state 16 mm PCD, 2 mm flange thickness or set-screw position. The 51105 is a 25/42/11 mm solid envelope without an exact supplier record. Evidence: scoped `parts/components.yaml:3`, `parts/components.yaml:11`, `parts/components.yaml:22`, `parts/compatibility.yaml:11`, and the shared model YAMLs. Confirm the actual items and dimensions before committing the final mating prints.

2. **Radial guidance, uplift strength and usable motion capacity.** The printed 24.9 mm spigot, 25.3 mm running bore, 0.5 mm uplift float and 2 mm retainer lip are geometry values, not load or wear validation. The thrust-bearing model is an undivided envelope and does not establish race fit/preload. Two fit coupons are now listed, but no coupon measurement or load/wear result exists. `loads.json` explicitly uses a 57 g legacy ball scenario and a generic motor curve; `requirements.yaml` asks for 600 g. Verify coupon fit, bearing seating, friction, temperature, creep, uplift and acceleration with the actual motor/current before assigning an operational load rating.

3. **Successful slicing and overhang acceptance.** Independent `printcheck` runs again reported `BambuStudio crashed (signal 6)` for all five new STEPs. Bed fit and minimum wall screening passed, but four assembly prints retain overhang flags: spindle 68.81 mm², pedestal 129.13 mm², adapter 11.78 mm² and retainer 92.01 mm². The written 8 mm bridge/counterbore/45° explanations are plausible; they need an actual slice preview and printed verification in the specified PETG orientation. The coupon's geometric `ok: true` does not mean slicing succeeded.

4. **Bench anchoring, wiring and real tool access.** Four 4.8 mm anchor holes are present through 55 mm legs, but support material, fastener type/length and pullout capacity are unspecified (`bom.csv`, `parts/build_plan.md:13`). There are no connector/cable or hex-key keepout solids in `assembly.yaml`; the coupling model omits the actual radial set screws. Open motor sides and the documented install-before-retainer sequence provide access in principle, but the actual plug, cable bend and key approach cannot be confirmed from these envelopes. Resolve these interfaces and the anchoring stack before operation.

5. **Inherited upper assembly and full motion release.** The preserved upper meshes are not included as solid bodies in the new-base boolean checks. `motion_screen.py:28` excludes turntable/base mesh nodes and screens vertex containment at 27 sampled poses, not all triangle intersections or continuous motion. Independent z bounds put the static main body at or above 25.697 mm and the upper motors at 29.698/29.702 mm, above the retainer top at 23.697 mm; this establishes that particular static separation only. The inherited calibrator STL has one boundary/nonmanifold edge in `checks/all_print_meshes.json`. The original 20T pulley geometry/BOM versus 16T parameter/5.625 ratio inconsistency also remains documented in scoped compatibility. These are preserved limitations, not regressions from the base change; they must be resolved or explicitly bounded for physical release.
