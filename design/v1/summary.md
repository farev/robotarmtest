# v1: 4-axis belt-driven desktop arm

## Layout
- J1 base yaw: J1 motor hangs under the base deck at +Y; GT2 20T → printed 120T on the turret hub, GT2-300 loop
  (C = 73.07 mm, motor slotted ±3 mm). 6:1. Turret turns on 2 × 6806-2RS in a tube under the deck.
- J2 shoulder: J2 motor rides on the upper arm behind the shoulder (counterweight), 300° from the intermediate
  shaft (below and forward). 20T → 60T on a 5 mm shaft (2 × 625 in a sliding carriage), 20T → printed 80T **fixed**
  to the tower: the pack climbs the fixed pulley. GT2-200 (C 58.62) + GT2-200 (C 46.04), one belt size. 12:1.
  The 300° placement gives every J2 motor screw hex-key access clear of the 60T and the stage-1 belt (the closest,
  inside the belt loop, has ~2 mm to the belt's inner face with a Ø6.5 key envelope; the 158 mm / 270° layout
  grazed the belt in the service-j2 check).
- J3 elbow: elbow motor also in the pack; 20T → printed 120T on the forearm, GT2-520 loop (C 187.30, slotted). 6:1.
  Relative joint (no J2/J3 coupling).
- J4 wrist roll: motor inside the forearm tip, generic 5 mm rigid flange coupling (Amazon, 22 mm flange, holes on a
  16 mm circle) straight to the gripper. 1:1. (Pololu 1998 hub dropped: not deliverable by Saturday.)
- Gripper: MG90S, m2 z15 gear pair (addendum 0.85), fingers with drop pads so the grip centre is on the roll axis.
- Tool plane y = 0 on the yaw axis (upper arm offset to −Y; offsets along the joint axes do not change planar IK).
- Shoulder axis 187 mm above the table, L2 = 135, elbow → pad centre 220. Reach ≈ 354 mm from the shoulder axis;
  the gripper works down to ~35 mm (pad centre) above the table at x ≈ 296 mm (J3 −43). Firmware numbers: control/joint_limits.yaml.
- Joint limits used: J1 ±150°, J2 −30…+90°, J3 −135…+120°, J4 ±180°. **Table limit (firmware, every pose):**
  h = 187 + 135·sin J2 + 220·sin(J2 + J3) ≥ 35 mm. The table is modelled as a keep-out in every pose check; the limit
  poses reach-table (0, 0, −43), shoulder-down (90, −30, 8) and soft-limit (0, 20, −84) are clear of table and base.

## Assembly check (2026-10-29, after the design-review fixes)
Pose variants in design/v1/poses/<name>/assembly.yaml (generated by make_assembly.py, checked by check_all.py):
home (0, 45, −90, 0), stretched, up, folded, reach-table, shoulder-down, elbow-up, yaw-150, plus two service poses:
`service-j2`: the upper arm alone on the bench, hex-key keep-outs on the 4 J2 motor screws (stage 1 is tensioned
and the J2 screws tightened here: on the tower, no shoulder angle from −30 to +90° gets them clear of the tower wall,
design/v1/scan_service.py); stage 2 is tensioned later by the carriage, which slides at right angles to the
stage-1 belt;
`service-tower`: turret + tower without the arm, key keep-outs on the 4 tower-foot screws. Results: see
check_results.json and the reply; every pose was clean except the items fixed below before the final run.

Modelled fasteners: 4 J2 motor screws (M3 × 8), 2 carriage clamps (M3 × 12), 4 tower-foot screws (M3 × 12),
shaft lock (M3 × 30, tip on the rod), with key keep-outs where they are tightened in place. Screw shanks are modelled
at the thread minor diameter so a centred screw clears its 3.6 hole.
Not modelled: the other screws (cap, clamp, deck, covers, motor screws of J1/J3/J4, gripper), inserts, cable paths,
motion between poses, end-stop mounts (not in v1), the servo horn.

Key-access notes: J2 motor screws are tightened on the bench (service-j2). Every modelled screw's key keep-out is
Ø6.5 × 60 mm (head Ø + 1, assembly skill).
Carriage clamps are tightened from inside the pack with the pack cover off.

### Allowed contacts (all by design)
Bearings in their press pockets (+0.1) and on shafts/spigot; spacers on the spigot and in the pocket; pulleys and the
hub on motor shafts; belts on their pulleys; rods in their 8.2/8.3 bores, collars and shims on rods; the intermediate
rod in its wall slot and carriage; gripper gears in mesh (0.1 backlash, checked separately: 0.0 mm³ overlap,
check_fingers.py); the idler finger on its axle boss; the servo in its 0.3 cut-out; the hub bolted to the gripper base;
J2 motor screws in the motor threads; each plug keep-out against its own motor; two face-clamped bolted joints
(tower/fixed pulley, forearm face plate/J4 motor: 0.03-0.06 mm³ boolean noise in rotated poses).
The motor-against-mount and Uno-against-shell allowances from the first version were removed (design review).

## Printability (printcheck: every part fits the 256 bed as modelled, no thin walls, no small holes)
Accepted overhangs, with reasons:
- base-shell ~90 mm²: tops of the USB window and vent slots (≤ 14 mm bridges in a 3 mm wall); the 11.3/22.3 panel
  holes are teardrops.
- turret-hub 85, shoulder-pulley-80t 107, forearm ~100 of its 207: 1.1 mm GT2 groove tops under the cone flanges.
- upper-arm ~216: 1.5 mm ledge of the bed-side 608 pocket (seats the outer ring, then a 45° cone) and the 6.6 mm
  bridges over the two captive-nut slots on the −Y face.
- forearm, pack-carriage: same 1.5 mm bearing ledges.
- gripper-base 67: 3.3 mm flat roofs of the four hex nut pockets and the Ø3.6 coupling-screw holes in the plate.
- gripper fingers 56: 1 mm grip-ridge ceilings on the pad faces.
- upper-arm nut-slot floor 1.3 mm: sandwiched between the nut and the clamped carriage (compression only).
- pack-carriage 625 pocket walls 4.45 / 2.7 to the counterbores (rule asks 0.4 × bore = 6.4): no room between the two
  motors; PETG part, light radial load (~180 N est on the inner bearing).
- shoulder-tower 2.5: teardrop rod bore; no lightening window (finger trap). The tower prints upright on its foot: the out-of-plane bending from the arm
  (~0.9 N·m on the 90 × 12 wall plus gussets, ~0.4 MPa est) crosses the layers but is far below PLA layer strength.
- coupon-fits 25: GT2 test stub groove tops.
Largest parts: upper-arm 231.7 × 155.6 × 55.8, base-shell / base-deck Ø230. Slicer not run (BambuStudio crashed in
the check tool).

## Design language
Soft, Jade-White body with orange drive parts (turret hub, fixed 80T, gripper fingers). R_OUTER 6, bed-edge chamfer
on the base shell, one drive: M3 socket heads (2.5 mm key) everywhere, M2 only on the servo. Values in style.py.
Open items for looks: bed-edge chamfers on the tower, arms and covers.

## Material
PLA, except base-deck, upper-arm, pack-cover, pack-carriage and forearm in **PETG** (they touch or sit beside a
stepper body). For the Saturday build everything is PLA at 0.6 A driver current; check the motor cases stay below
~45 °C after 10 minutes, and reprint those five parts in PETG later.
