## Should fix

1. **Soft limits and the table (new)**: the documented soft limit lets the gripper go deep into the table. `check` cannot catch this because the table is not modelled.
   - Pad-centre height = Z_SH + L2·sin q2 + 220·sin(q2 + q3), with Z_SH 187, L2 135 and elbow → pad 220 (layout.py, joint_limits.yaml:16-18).
   - Pose `soft-limit` (0, −30, −30) is called "clear" in joint_limits.yaml:20-21 and summary.md:19-21. Its pad is at 187 − 67.5 − 190.5 = **−71 mm**, at x = 116.9 + 110 = 227, which is outside the base (r 115), so the pad is inside the table.
   - Pose `reach-table` (0, 0, −60): in poses/reach-table/assembly.yaml:585-596 the coupling face is placed at (207.5, 61.43). The pad centre, 75 further on, is at z = 61.43 − 75·0.866 = **−3.5**, and the pad's lower edge is at about −6. make_assembly.py:265 says "pads ~11 mm above the table". That was true with the old shoulder height of 201 (201 − 190.5).
   - The rule "only when J2 < 0" also misses J2 ≥ 0. For example, (0, 0, −90) puts the pad at 187 − 220 = −33.
   - Fix: make the firmware limit a table constraint: 187 + 135 sin q2 + 223 sin(q2 + q3) ≥ 10, which is about q3 ≥ −53° at J2 = 0. Or add a table keep-out slab (z −20..0, r 400) to the poses and re-check. Then correct joint_limits.yaml:20-21, summary.md:19-21 and the reach-table comment.
2. **Thread engagement in the motor threads**: engagement is at or below the rule, and the written justification does not hold.
   - J2, J3 and J4 use M3 × 8 through 4 mm plates, which engages 4.0 mm. Build-notes §2 cites machine_elements.md §7.3, "steel ≥ 1 d". But the same line (machine_elements.md:716) says "aluminium ≥ 1.5–2 d", and NEMA 17 front end caps are aluminium. So 4.0 is below 4.5.
   - J1 uses M3 × 10 + 1 washer through the 5 mm deck, which engages 4.5. That equals the *estimated* thread depth of 4.5 ± 0.5 (nema17-42shdc.yaml:11), so there is zero margin before the screw bottoms.
   - Fix: extend the "screw an M3 into a motor hole and measure" step in build-notes §2 to J1. If the depth is under 5 mm, use 2 washers on J1: the heads now sit clear of the belt in XY, so the extra 0.5 mm costs nothing.
3. **Hex-key keep-outs are Ø5; the assembly skill asks for head Ø + 1 = 6.5** (m3-key-keepout.yaml:10). I checked the Ø6.5 case by hand, and every tightened screw still clears by more than 0.5 mm:
   - J1 (0, 51.15): 47.9 to the 120T flange (r 39.2), 14.4 to the belt runs at x ±17.7.
   - J1 (±21.92, 73.07): 10.7 to the 20T flange.
   - J2 screw (−32.23, −35.26): it sits inside the stage-1 loop, about 2.1 mm from the belt's inner face.
   - Tower foot: 4.25 to the gussets.
   - Carriage: Ø6.5 equals its counterbore.
   - Enlarge the keep-out model so `check` covers this.
4. **pack-carriage 625 pocket walls (§4)**: unchanged. The pocket wall is 4.45 against the required 6.44, and the pocket-to-counterbore wall is 14 − 3.25 − 8.05 = 2.70 against 3.0 (pack-carriage.py:16; printcheck min wall 2.13). summary.md:75 now gives a reason.
