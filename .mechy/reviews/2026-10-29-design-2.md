## Blocking

1. **Carriage clamp nuts (stage-2 tension)**: the captive-nut slots are wide enough for the M3 nut to spin, so the two carriage clamp screws cannot be tightened.
   - The slot width is `nut_w / cos(30°)` = 5.8 / 0.866 = 6.70 mm (upper-arm.py:127-130). A section of upper-arm.step at print z = 1.35 measures both nut slots at **6.697 mm**.
   - An M3 nut is 5.5 across flats and 6.01 min / 6.35 nominal across corners (fasteners_hex_nuts.csv), so it turns freely in a 6.70 slot. §2 asks for a side-entry slot of s + 0.3 = 5.8.
   - You cannot hold the nut either. The slots sit 14 ± 3 mm from the intermediate shaft, under the 60T flange (r 21) and hub (1 mm off the wall). Build-notes §3.5 tensions stage 2 with those pulleys already fitted.
   - The screw also barely clears the nut. The M3 x 12 tip is at y -81.0 (placed bbox), and the nut seated on the slot floor spans -78.5..-80.9. That leaves 0.1 mm protruding, while §2 asks for at least 1 pitch (0.5).
   - Fix: make the slot 5.8 wide (drop the `/cos(30°)`), and deepen `CARR_CBORE` (pack-carriage.py:17) by 0.5 so the tip protrudes 0.6. It then still clears the 60T hub face at -82.2 by 0.7.
2. **J1 motor screws under the J1 belt**: as the build notes specify them, the two inner J1 screw heads sit inside the running belt, and a key cannot reach them once the belt is on.
   - Build-notes §2 calls for "M3 × 10 + 2 × M3 washers" on the deck top. That puts the head at z 61..64. The belt spans z 63.7..69.7 (centre 66.7, 20T teeth 63.2..70.2).
   - At the inner holes (±15.5, 57.57), the belt pitch line is at x = (38.2 − 0.4356·57.57)/0.9001 = 14.6. The head spans x 12.75..18.25, so it sits under the belt.
   - Scratch check (home pose plus 4 × m3x10-shcs at the J1 holes; nothing in the project changed):
     - with washers: `belt-j1 / screw-j1-1` and `belt-j1 / screw-j1-2` interfere, 2.261 mm³ each;
     - with the Ø5 key keep-outs, the keep-outs also cross the belt: 38.9 mm³ with washers, 40.9 mm³ without.
   - Without washers the heads clear the belt, but M3 x 10 through the 5 mm deck then engages 5.0 in a thread estimated at 4.5 deep (nema17-42shdc.yaml), so it bottoms out.
   - Build-notes §3.1 loops the belt and then says "Slide the J1 motor outward to tension; tighten its screws". The inner pair cannot be reached at that point. The J1 motor screws are not modelled (summary.md "Not modelled ... motor screws of J1/J3/J4"), which is why `check` passed.
   - Fix (re-check after either): rotate the J1 motor 45° about its axis. The screws then sit on the belt centreline (for example (0, 51.15): the belt runs are at x ±17.7 there) and clear the turret plate circle (r 43.1). Or tighten before looping the belt, with heads that clear the belt by at least 0.5 mm and a length that engages at least 4.5 without bottoming. Model the screws and key keep-outs either way.

## Should fix

1. **Thread engagement below 1.5 × d (§2)**: four joints engage only 4.0 mm, below 4.5.
   - J1: M3 x 10 + 2 washers through the 5 mm deck.
   - J2, J3, J4: M3 x 8 through the 4 mm plates.
   - build-notes §2 justifies this as "≥ 1 d in steel, machine_elements §7.3". That reference is not in this project's skills, and the rule in fdm_pla_rules.md §2 is 1.5 × d.
   - Fix: thin the plates locally or counterbore the heads.
2. **Re-tensioning the J2 drive needs a teardown**:
   - Stage 1 can only be set on the bench: summary.md says the J2 screws are behind the tower wall at every shoulder angle (scan_service.py).
   - Stage 2 needs the pack cover, the J3 motor and the J3 belt off.
   - decisions.md:8 accepts PLA everywhere for the deadline, and §8 says clamped and preloaded PLA creeps ("re-tension after a week").
   - Write the re-tension procedure in build-notes, or give one stage an adjuster you can reach in place.
3. **Hex-key keep-outs smaller than the assembly skill's**:
   - The skill asks for head Ø + 1 = 6.5 mm. m3-key-keepout is Ø5.0 (inspect bbox ±2.5), and m3-key-keepout-narrow is Ø3.5 (±1.75). The narrow one is used for J2 screws 2 and 4 (poses/service-j2/assembly.yaml:235-246, 283-294).
   - The two carriage clamp screws, which are tightened on the tower, have no keep-out in any pose (home or service-j2).
   - By hand: from +Y their key path clears the J2 body (screws at UA z ±7, body top z -29.6), and the elbow motor is off with the cover.
4. **pack-carriage 625 pocket walls (§4)**:
   - With `CH` = 25 the pocket wall is (25 − 16.1)/2 = 4.45, against max(3.0, 0.4 × 16.1 = 6.44).
   - The pocket-to-counterbore wall is 14 − 3.25 − 8.05 = 2.70, below 3.0.
   - Better than before (2.95), and the part is PETG, but the rule still fails.
5. **Nut-slot floor 1.3 mm**: the 4.0 wall minus the 2.7 nut slot leaves 1.3 mm (printcheck upper-arm min_wall_mm 1.3). This floor carries the clamp load, and §4 asks for 2.0 on a load-bearing wall. Fix it together with Blocking 1.
6. **Hardware list (parts/components.yaml:83)**:
   - It lists "M3 nuts x 3". Build-notes §2 needs 7: 4 behind the coupling flange, 2 captive carriage nuts and 1 jam nut.
   - The 8 washers are for the J1 stack. Update that count if Blocking 2 drops the washers.
   - parts/build_plan.md:25 still lists a "DIN 9021 M3 washer". The design now uses a 12 mm OD fender washer.
   - The screw total does match: §2 gives 19 × M3x10, 26 × M3x8, 10 × M3x12, 1 × M3x30 and 1 × M3x6, which is 57.
7. **Out-of-date or conflicting documents**:
