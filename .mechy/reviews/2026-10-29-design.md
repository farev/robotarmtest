## Blocking

1. **shoulder-tower to turret-hub (4 foot screws)**: none of the 4 foot screws can be fitted as M3 x 10 from the foot top.
   - The two inner holes sit under the 12 mm wall. `FOOT_INSERTS` y = Y0 + 6 = -121.4 (shoulder-tower.py:19, turret-hub.py:23), and the wall spans y -127.4..-115.4 with x ±45 at the foot (shoulder-tower.py:37-41). `inspect shoulder-tower.step` shows these holes running up through the wall: (-36, -121.4) is 59.19 deep and (36, -121.4) is 85.79 deep, from the bed. The screw head has no seat, and a 60-86 mm screw would be needed.
   - The two outer holes at (±36, -106) land on the gussets. The gussets span x ±(37.5..42.5) (`GUSSET_X` ±40, `GUSSET_T` 5, lines 22-23, 43-47) and are about 15.5 mm tall at y = -106. An M3 head (Ø5.5 SHCS, Ø5.7 button) at x = 36 reaches 38.75, so it sits 1.25 mm into the gusset. `inspect` also shows the 3.6 hole nicking the gusset (24.08 deep instead of 6).
   - Fix: move the foot inserts outside the wall and gussets (for example y = -109 and y = -133 with a longer foot, or x = ±30 between the gussets on the +Y side), or counterbore the heads.
2. **Shoulder shaft lock (M3 x 25 into the tower head insert)**: the screw stops 1.0 mm above the rod. Evidence: `inspect shoulder-tower.step` puts the head top at z 144.8 (print frame; 4.0 insert hole centred at 141.45, 6.7 deep), the Ø8.2 bore axis at z 114.8, so the rod top is at 118.8. From the seat to the rod is 144.8 - 118.8 = 26.0 mm, and an M3 x 25 tip lands at 119.8 (build-notes.md §2 "Tower shaft lock (bears on the rod) M3 x 25"). The rod is then held only by the collars. Fix: M3 x 30 (sticks out 4 mm), or lower the insert; and see Should fix 5 for the Ø3.0 passage.
3. **J2 drive tensioning: carriage clamp screws and one J2 motor screw cannot be reached**:
   - The build order (build-notes.md §3.6) tensions the belts after both pulley stages are on: "slide the carriage rearward (stage 2), clamp, then slide the J2 motor down its slots (stage 1)".
   - Carriage screws: the 2 x M3 x 10 heads sit on the -Y wall outer face (y -81.2..-84.2), 12.5 mm from the intermediate shaft (placed insert holes (-41.4, 159.6) and (-23.7, 177.3); shaft axis (-32.55, 168.45)). The 60T hub, modelled Ø20 at y -81.7..-88.7 (gt2-60t-5mm.yaml), overlaps the head annulus (r 9.75..15.25) by 0.25 mm over 2.5 mm of depth. The Ø42 flange and Ø37.7 teeth also cover the screws, so no hex key can reach them.
   - J2 motor screw: the placed hole at (-17.0, 157.0) is 19.3 mm from the 60T axis. Its head spans r 16.6..22.1 under the Ø42 flange (r 21; Olimex lists Ø44), with 4.5 mm between the head top and the flange. Over the ±3 mm slot travel the distance stays 16.7..22.0 mm.
   - The check missed this because no screws or hex-key keep-outs are modelled.
   - Fix: put the carriage clamp screws outside the 60T flange radius (≥ 25 mm from the shaft, or from inside the pack into nut traps). Rotate the J2 motor pattern or the J2 angle so no motor screw falls within the 60T flange radius + 3 mm. Then add the screws and hex-key keep-outs and re-check.

## Should fix

1. **Assembly method (fasteners, tool access, allow_contact)**:
   - No screw, insert or hex-key/iron keep-out is modelled (summary.md "Not modelled": "Screws and heat-set inserts"). The assembly skill (step 4-5) requires them at every critical joint, and their absence is how Blocking 1-3 passed `check`.
   - Five allow_contact pairs allow a motor or board against its whole mount: motor-j1/base-deck, motor-j2/upper-arm, motor-j3/pack-cover, motor-j4/forearm, uno-cnc-shield/base-shell (assembly.yaml:693-700, 789-790). The skill says "allow the screw against the motor, not the mount against the motor". Face contact at 0 needs no allowance, and the pilot is Ø22 in a Ø23 slot (0.5 radial).
   - These pairs hide body collisions. Example by hand: at full +3 mm slot travel along 250°, the J2 motor bottom (z -58.55, UA frame) clears the pack's lower-left insert boss top (z -58.73) by 0.18 mm, and the tool never checks it.
   - Fix: remove the five allowances, add screw models and keep-outs, re-run check and the 7 poses.
2. **Thread engagement below 1.5 x d (rules §2)**:
   - Motors: M3 x 8 through the 4 mm plates engages 4.0 < 4.5 (build-notes.md §2 admits "4 of 4.5"). The J1 motor goes through the 5 mm deck (`DECK_T` = 5, layout.py:118), so M3 x 8 engages only 3.0 mm. The build notes wrongly say "4 mm plates" for all four.
   - Uno: M3 x 6 through the 1.6 mm PCB engages 4.4. M3 x 8 fits (≤ 1.6 + 6.7 = 8.3).
   - Gripper to hub: gripper-base.py:1 says M3 x 10 and build-notes says M3 x 8. Only M3 x 10 engages ≥ 4.5 in the 5 mm through-tapped hub (it sticks out 1 mm behind the hub, into free space).
   - Fix: thin the motor plates at the screw slots, or counterbore, so a stock length engages ≥ 4.5 without bottoming in the 4.5 mm motor threads. Correct the notes.
3. **forearm cover inserts**: the wall outside the insert is 1.5 mm, below the 2.0 minimum (§3). `inspect forearm.step`: inserts at ±21.15, so the hole edge is at 23.15, and the side wall's outer face is at `ZW_OUT` 24.65 (forearm.py:18-23). The 8 x 8 boss (centre 21.15 ± 4 = 25.15) also sticks out 0.5 mm past the outer wall face at x = 30 and 70. Fix: move the inserts to ±20.65 and keep the bosses inside the wall.
4. **pack-carriage 625 pockets**: the wall is (22 - 16.1)/2 = 2.95 mm in z (pack-carriage.py:14, `CH` 22). §4 asks for max(3.0, 0.4 x 16.1 = 6.44). The part is PLA. It carries the overhung stage-2 load: the 20T belt plane is 35.5 mm outboard of the inner 625, and the bearings are 11 mm apart. About 42 N of working belt pull (1.08 N·m / 4 / 6.37 mm) gives about 178 N on the inner bearing, plus pre-tension (estimate). Fix: CH ≥ 29, or PETG and a wider bearing spacing.
5. **Tower horizontal fits**: the shoulder bore is a horizontal teardrop at 8.2 (shoulder-tower.py:49). §1 says horizontal holes add +0.1, so 8.3. Coupon row D is printed vertical, so it does not test this hole. The shaft-lock passage under the insert is Ø3.0 (line 54). A vertical hole prints about 0.2 small, so the M3 would cut 19 mm of thread into it; make it 3.6.
6. **base-shell panel holes**: the Ø22.3 E-stop and Ø11.3 jack holes are plain round holes in the vertical wall (base-shell.py:67-68). §5 requires a teardrop or flat top above Ø8, and a sagging roof makes the 22.3 cut-out undersize. The written overhang reason is out of date: summary.md says "12/16 mm panel holes", 156 mm²; printcheck measures 166.56 mm².
7. **shoulder-tower print orientation**: the foot and wall are printed upright as an L (144.8 mm tall). The arm's weight offset along the rod (about 14 N at about 60 mm) bends the wall about X, across the layers. §6 says an upright L-bracket peels at the corner. Estimated stress is low (0.86 N·m on a 90 x 12 section is about 0.4 MPa, before the gussets), so this is not blocking, but a reason needs writing or the part re-orienting.
8. **Stage-2 20T to tower face**: the outer flange (y -114.7) passes 0.7 mm from the tower's +Y face (y -115.4) wherever the pulley orbits over the wall (`up` pose). The pulley length is an estimate (16 ± 1 mm, gt2-20t-5mm.yaml), and the set screw position is set by hand ("1 mm beyond the 60T"). Fix: 2 mm or more of design clearance.
9. **Gripper idler retention**: a DIN 9021 M3 washer (OD 9) over the Ø8.4 gear bore overlaps the gear by only 0.3 mm radially. The axle boss stands 0.2 mm above the gear (gripper-base.py:46, gripper-finger-right.py:17). Fix: a printed cap or M3 x 12 OD washer, or a stepped boss.
10. **Function gaps**:
    - The J4 motor cable and the servo cable have no route across the elbow and shoulder (build-notes §3.10 covers only the pack cables).
    - The 3 end-stop switches are on the parts list, and the notes home J1-J3 with them, but v1 has no mounts.
    - Poses inside the stated joint limits hit the base (summary: "shoulder -30 / elbow -60 collides with the base shell"), so safe use depends on firmware soft limits that are not written anywhere yet.
11. **Hardware missing from parts/components.yaml**: 2 x M2 x 8 + 2 x M2 nuts, 1 x DIN 9021 M3 washer and 4 x M5 table screws exist only as notes or in build-notes. 15 M3 button heads (deck 6, pack cover 5, forearm cover 4) are called for, but the VXB kit lists "hex socket head" only.
12. **Out-of-date documents and models**:
    - summary.md says "m1.5 z20 gear pair"; the code uses m2 z15 (gripper_layout.py:11).
    - summary.md, decisions.md, layout.py:13, gripper_layout.py:1 and build-notes say "5 mm flange coupling". The assembly and components use the Pololu 1998 hub.
    - build-notes says "Panel jack (12 mm hole), switch (16 mm hole)"; the CAD has 11.3 and 22.3.
    - flange-coupling-5mm, rod-8x90 and rod-5x60 are unused model files.
13. **Print files**: print_list.csv has no rows for coupon-fits and coupon-yaw, although build-notes §1 says to print them first. Their STLs exist (no stale part STLs). The coupons test 3 sizes (-0.1/0/+0.1) instead of the 4 in §9 (-0.1..+0.2).
14. **Overhangs with no written reason**: gripper-base 24.97 mm² and coupon-fits 25.31 mm² (printcheck).
15. **Looks (legible_cad §5)**:
    - Two drives are used: M3 SHCS take a 2.5 mm key and M3 button heads a 2.0 mm key, while style.py says `DRIVE = "M3 hex"`, one drive.
    - Literal radii appear outside style.py: pack-carriage.py:31 (2.0) and gripper-base.py:32 (4.0).
    - The edge treatment is mixed: the tower, upper arm and forearm have hard edges, and only base-shell gets the `C_BED` chamfer (§5 asks for 0.4 chamfers on bed edges).
    - Open holes in the 10.2-35.6 mm finger-trap range sit beside running belts: upper-arm web Ø18/Ø20/Ø14 and forearm floor Ø15 x 2.
    - Dead `if False` code remains (turret-hub.py:40, upper-arm.py:48).
16. **Assembly order**: the turret-clamp screws can only be reached from inside the base, so the deck, bearings, turret and J1 have to be built as a sub-assembly before the deck is screwed to the shell. build-notes §3 never says when the 6 deck screws go in.

## Unverified

1. **Stepper body length 40 and shaft length 24** (nema17-42shdc.yaml: "estimate ... user to measure"). These set the 1 mm axial gap in the motor pack, the J1 pulley seat (needs ≥ 23 mm above the face), and the J4 hub engagement. Measured from the placed models: the shaft tip is at 144.0 and the hub spans 140-145, so 4 mm of shaft is in the hub. The motors are owned: measure them with calipers before printing the upper arm, pack-cover and forearm.
2. **Bought pulley envelopes** (20T/60T hub Ø, flange Ø, length, all "estimate"). They decide Blocking 3 (hub against the carriage screws) and Should fix 8. Confirm from the chosen listing or with calipers.
3. **Bearing ring lands**: the 6806 inner-ring OD is taken as ~33.5 under the Ø33 turret step, clamp and spacer, and the 608 inner-ring OD as ~12.1 under the Ø11.5 shims. Both are "[est]". Confirm from the bearing datasheet (d1).
4. **MG90S clone and horn**: horn height 3.5, horn screw radii 8/11.5 and pocket 5.3 x 15 x 1.8 are all "[est]" (gripper_layout.py:22, gripper-finger-left.py:15-18). Measure when the servo arrives.
5. **E-stop and panel jack inside the base**: neither is modelled. The IDEC datasheet link in components.yaml did not load (the page gave only the file id). The E-stop axis is 16.3 mm from the deck boss at 150°: the boss edge is 11.3 mm from the axis, 4.8 mm behind the wall's inner face. A locking ring or contact block wider than about Ø22 there would hit it, and the wall is curved (R112, about 1 mm sag over Ø31). Confirm with the IDEC HW drawing and add keep-outs.
6. **Slicer**: printcheck's slice step failed on all 20 STEPs ("BambuStudio crashed (signal 11)"). Geometry checks passed, but no slice confirms them.
7. **Stiffness**: rough estimates only. The 6 mm PLA turret plate spans about 61 mm from the neck to the tower foot, and at full reach a 2.1 N·m twist turns it about 0.8° (about 5 mm at the tip). The dead shoulder rod is held in about 37 mm of Ø8.2 PLA bores (0.2 mm play, about 0.3°). A deflection calculation or a test print would confirm.
