2. **Wiring and consumables not covered.** None of these is in components.yaml:

   - spiral wrap and zip ties (research/gt-invention-studio-store.md:15: the store's zip-tie bundle "is **out of
     stock**"), heat-shrink and threadlocker (build_plan.md:26, 29);
   - crimp housings and pins plus a crimp tool. build-notes.md:109 says "pass bare wires, crimp the shield end
     afterwards" because the cables' connectors cannot pass the turret's Ø12 bore: an XH 4-pin housing is about 12.4 mm
     long and a PH 6-pin about 13.9 mm.

   List each as owned (studio) or bought.
3. **Filament not covered.** About 830 g PLA (build-notes.md:4), plus PETG for the motor parts. No item and no `owned`
   entry.
4. **Spares short of the build notes.** Inserts: components.yaml:72 has qty 35, which equals the exact need, but
   build-notes.md:46 says "Total 35 inserts (buy 50)". Washers: components.yaml:83 lists 4, but build-notes.md:61 calls
   for "2 washers on J1" on each of 4 screws when the motor thread is under 5 mm, which is 8.
5. **build_plan.md is out of date with the parts list.** Line 22 says "16 mm latching switch", "7.5 A inline fuse" and a
   "12 mm hole". The parts list and CAD use a 22 mm E-stop (22.3 hole), a 10 A fuse (components.yaml:308,
   order-list.md:23) and an 11.3 mm jack hole (base-shell.py:69). The 10 A fuse is above the PSU's 6 A and above the
   jack's own rating (components.yaml:282 quotes "0.5-3 A" for this jack type), so it protects neither. Use the plan's
   7.5 A, or 5 A for an est. 2-3 A draw.
6. **Driver current and torque margins disagree between files.** compatibility.yaml:5 sets 0.7 A (Vref 0.56 V).
   build-notes.md:11 and 121 set **0.6 A** (Vref 0.48 V) while the motor parts are PLA, and decisions.md:15 accepts PLA
   for the deadline. Holding torque scales roughly with current, so at 0.6 A: 0.34 × 0.6/0.9 = 0.227 N·m. J2 needs 1.18 /
   12 / 0.90 = 0.109 N·m (48 % of that, not the 32 % in compatibility.yaml:125). J3 needs 0.57 / 6 / 0.95 = 0.100 N·m
   (44 %, not 29 %). decisions.md:24 quotes 29 % and 30 %, a third set of numbers. These are still under 50 %, but they
   rest on holding torque taken from a sibling motor (unverified, item U3). Update the checks to the current you will
   actually set.
7. **MG90S checks are marked `verified` on evidence that does not say it.** The part bought is a Miuzei MG90S (store
   product 6265), but the datasheet is TowerPro's. The TowerPro page says only "Operating voltage: 4.8V". It gives no
   4.8-6.6 V range, no ~0.8 A stall current and no 50 Hz 1-2 ms PWM, all of which compatibility.yaml:55 and 60 claim from

9. **The E-stop drops the arm.** build-notes.md:126: the "NC contacts in series with +24 V to the shield". Cutting motor
